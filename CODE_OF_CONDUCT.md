# Code of conduct

RescueLoop should be a place where people can work together without having to prove they belong.

Be respectful. Discuss the work, not the person. Assume mistakes are ordinary, explain disagreement
clearly, and leave room for people to change their minds. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexual attention, and sustained personal attacks are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break these
rules. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report conduct that should not be handled in public, contact the maintainer through the private
contact options on the GitHub profile for [ostapondo](https://github.com/ostapondo). Reports will be
reviewed as privately and promptly as practical.

This code applies in the repository and in other spaces where someone is representing RescueLoop.
